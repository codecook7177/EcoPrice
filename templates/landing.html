// 01 ARCHITECTURE & PURPOSE

Eliminating perishable spoilage in modern retail.

Perishable inventory decay represents a staggering retail loss and landfill burden. EcoPrice dynamically bridges shelf-life expiration with localized consumer demand elasticity.

01.01 // MARKET DEFECT

Rigid Pricing Inefficiency

Traditional grocers rely on manual, delayed markdowns that happen hours before expiration. Uniform price slashes either erode profit margins needlessly or fail to clear stock before safety thresholds expire.

Rs.44K Cr ANNUAL RETAIL PERISHABLE LOSS
01.02 // DUAL-TIER ENGINE

Causal Prediction & RL

EcoPrice unites supervised gradient boosting with an autonomous Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) reinforcement learning agent. The agent dynamically identifies the Pareto frontier between revenue and waste penalties.

85.7% MEASURED SPOILAGE DECELERATION
01.03 // RETAIL OUTCOME

Automated Margin Protection

Price recommendations are generated based on days-to-expiry, local weather variations, and batch volume surplus. Store managers maintain complete oversight or allow autonomous high-confidence adjustments.

+5.5% NET RECOVERY ON EXPIRING ASSETS
// 02 OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW

System Architecture in Brief.

How inventory telemetry transforms from physical batch expiration into verified checkout register discount tags across four stages.

01
INPUT PIPELINE

Data Ingestion

Aggregates point-of-sale logs, inventory batch timestamps, category shelf lives, and meteorological conditions into daily tensors.

02
XGBOOST REGRESSOR

Demand Forecasting

Evaluates 53 engineered features to predict customer purchase volumes per item with 86.5% R² causal accuracy across retail benchmarks.

03
PPO RL AGENT

Discount Optimization

Balances revenue against spoilage penalty λ and markdown penalty γ to calculate the optimal price reduction percentage.

04
REGISTER SYNC

Manager Verification

Discounts populate the manager ledger for one-click verification, automated barcode price tag printing, and POS checkout updates.

MODEL ARCHITECTURE XGBoost 3.4 + Stable-Baselines3 PPO
